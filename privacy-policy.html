PixelApp — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

PixelApp is a personal travel and expense management application designed to help users organize trips, expenses, budgets, locations, plans, and travel-related photos.

1. Information we store

Depending on the features you use, PixelApp may store:

  • Trip information and travel dates
  • Expenses, amounts, categories and notes
  • Budget information
  • Fuel and mileage information
  • Saved places and travel locations
  • Trip plans and tasks
  • Photos uploaded by the user
  • Application settings and preferences

2. Google Account and Google Drive

PixelApp may use Google OAuth to allow the user to connect their Google Drive account.

When the user authorizes Google Drive access, PixelApp uses the authorization to store and synchronize the user's PixelApp data and travel photos in the user's Google Drive.

PixelApp does not sell, rent, or transfer the user's Google Drive data to third parties.

Google authentication is handled by Google. PixelApp does not receive or store the user's Google account password.

3. How information is used

The information stored by PixelApp is used only to provide the application's functionality, including:

  • Managing trips and expenses
  • Calculating budgets and travel statistics
  • Synchronizing application data
  • Storing and displaying travel photos
  • Displaying saved travel locations and plans
  • Providing backups and restoring application data

4. Data sharing

PixelApp does not sell personal information.

PixelApp does not share the user's travel data, expenses, photos, or Google Drive data with third parties except where technically necessary to provide services explicitly used by the user, such as Google authentication and Google Drive synchronization.

5. Data storage

Application data may be stored locally by the application and/or in the user's connected Google Drive account.

The user remains responsible for the contents of their Google Drive account and may manage or delete their stored PixelApp data through Google Drive.

6. Google API Services

PixelApp's use of information received from Google APIs complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements where applicable.

PixelApp uses Google Drive access only for the functionality described above.

7. Data deletion

The user can delete PixelApp data stored in their connected Google Drive account.

The user can also revoke PixelApp's Google account access through their Google Account security settings.

8. Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect application data and authentication information. However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application's functionality or data practices change.

The latest version will always be published at the Privacy Policy address associated with PixelApp.
